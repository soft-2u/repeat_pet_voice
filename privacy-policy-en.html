Overview FurBox (also distributed as FurBox: Pet Voice and FurBox: Pet Talk & Training) is a free mobile application developed by 2U Software (Soft2u). The application provides a pet voice repeater (listening and repeating dog and cat sounds), a prank soundboard for dogs and cats, and pet training tools including a clicker box and an ultrasonic training whistle[cite: 2, 3, 4, 5]. 2. Microphone Permission & Local AI Audio Processing[cite: 1, 2, 4, 5] Microphone Access: The application requires Microphone permission in order to listen to, detect, record, and repeat your pet's sounds[cite: 2, 4, 5]. You can grant or revoke this permission at any time in your device's Settings[cite: 2, 4, 5]. On-Device AI Filtering (YAMNet): FurBox uses an AI filter powered by YAMNet to ignore human speech and background noise so that the app only repeats dog and cat sounds[cite: 1, 2, 4, 5]. No Audio Uploaded to Servers: All audio processing and YAMNet AI filtering take place locally (offline) directly on your device[cite: 1]. The application does not collect, store on external servers, or upload your voice or any audio from your microphone to any server[cite: 1]. 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)[cite: 1, 4, 5, 6] AdMob Integration: FurBox is a free application and generates revenue through advertisements provided by Google AdMob[cite: 1, 5]. The app displays banner ads, interstitial ads, native ads, and video ads (such as rewarded video ads to unlock premium clicker tools)[cite: 4, 5, 6]. Data Collected by Third Parties: Our third-party advertising partner (Google AdMob) may use device identifiers (such as your device's Advertising ID) and diagnostic data to serve and manage advertisements[cite: 1]. 4.
