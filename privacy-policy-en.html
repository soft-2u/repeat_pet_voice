1. Overview

FurBox (also distributed under the names FurBox: Pet Voice and FurBox: Pet Talk & Training) is a mobile application developed by 2U Software (Soft2u). The application provides a Pet Voice Assistant ("Listen & Repeat") that records and repeats dog and cat sounds, a Prank Soundboard for dogs and cats, and pet Training Tools including a Clicker Box and an Ultrasonic Training Whistle.

2. Microphone Permission & Local AI Audio Processing

  • Microphone Access: The application requires Microphone permission to listen for, detect, record, and repeat your pet's sounds. You may grant or revoke this permission at any time in your device's Settings.
  • On-Device AI Filter (YAMNet): FurBox uses an AI filter powered by YAMNet to ignore human speech and background noise so that the application only repeats dog and cat sounds.
  • No Audio Uploaded to External Servers: The application does not collect or upload your voice or any audio from your microphone to any server. All audio processing, recording, repeating, and YAMNet AI filtering take place locally on your device.
Key Privacy Commitment: Your microphone audio never leaves your device. All voice detection and AI filtering (YAMNet) run 100% locally on your phone.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • AdMob Monetization: FurBox integrates Google AdMob to generate revenue and keep the application free for users (including banner ads, interstitial ads, native ads, and video ads used to unlock premium clicker tools).
  • Device Identifiers & Diagnostic Data: Our third-party advertising partner (Google AdMob) may use device identifiers (such as your device's Advertising ID) and diagnostic data to serve and manage advertisements.

4. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — EEA Users

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the following GDPR provisions apply to your use of the application:

  • Local Audio Processing: As stated above, the application does not collect or upload any voice or audio data from your microphone to any server; all audio processing and YAMNet AI filtering are performed locally on your device.
  • User Consent for Advertising: Our third-party partner (Google AdMob) may use device identifiers (Advertising ID) and diagnostic data to display advertisements based on your Consent (managed via the Google UMP SDK consent prompt).

5. Digital Services Act (DSA) Notice for EU Users

In compliance with the European Union's Digital Services Act (DSA), because the application incorporates AdMob advertising to generate revenue, the developer is classified as a Trader on Google Play Console. Verified trader information — including address, telephone number, and support email — is transparently displayed on the application's Google Play Store listing for users in the European Union.

6. App Preferences, In-App Updates & Safety Notice

  • App Preferences & Updates: FurBox allows you to customize your display language and visual theme (Light, Dark, or System Default), and supports downloading and installing in-app updates.
  • Pet Safety Notice: When using the Prank Soundboard or the Training Whistle (8–10 kHz, 12–15 kHz, and 18–20 kHz presets), please keep your speaker at a moderate volume. Do not place the phone speaker directly against your pet's ears at maximum volume.
